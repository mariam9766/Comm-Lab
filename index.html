Trapped

About

Just like a dream... a young girl finds herself in an unfamiliar space, completely alone, and desperately searching for a way out. Running around in fear and stuck in loops, she finds one single person, sitting alone and speaking gibberish. Filled with anxiety and hallucinations, she continues to run, stuck between what is real and what is not.
